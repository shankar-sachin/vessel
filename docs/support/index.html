Support

Help with Vessel

Most answers are already in the wiki. If yours isn’t, here’s how to reach the developer.

Start with the wiki

The Vessel wiki covers every screen, and its troubleshooting section answers the questions that come up most:

  • Vessel logged the wrong food
  • Voice, microphone and speech messages
  • My streak didn’t fill
  • Why no patterns appear yet
  • Backing up and moving to a new phone
  • Apple Watch entries not reaching the phone

Contact

For a bug, a question or a suggestion, open an issue on GitHub. You’ll need a free GitHub account. Every issue is read.

What helpsYour device and iOS version (Settings → General → About), the Vessel version (at the bottom of Vessel’s Settings), what you did, and what happened instead. For a wrong parse, the exact sentence you typed.
Keep your diary privateIssues are public. Don’t paste your exported diary or anything about your health you wouldn’t post openly. A sentence that reproduces the problem is enough.

Medical questions

Vessel can’t give medical advice, and neither can its support. If a reaction is severe, sudden, or comes with difficulty breathing, swelling, chest pain or blood, get medical help straight away.

Versions

Release notes for every version are on GitHub Releases. The current release is Vessel 2.0.
