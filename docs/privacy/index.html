Privacy policy

Vessel collects nothing

Your diary stays on your device. Here’s exactly what that means, including the one thing that does leave it.

Effective 22 September 2026. This policy covers the Vessel app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, developed by Sachin Shankar.

The short version

Vessel does not collect your data. Everything you log stays on your device. There is no account, no Vessel server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives anything you enter.

What Vessel stores, and where

Your meals, drinks, water, journal entries, moods, reactions, goals and settings are stored in the app’s own storage on your device. They’re included in your device’s normal iCloud or computer backup if you have that turned on, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

If you choose a backup folder, Vessel writes a copy of your diary there. If that folder is in iCloud Drive or another cloud provider, the copy is stored by that provider under their terms. You choose the folder, and you can stop at any time.

If you export your diary, the file goes wherever you send it.

What leaves your device

Barcode lookups. When you look up a barcode, Vessel sends that barcode number to Open Food Facts, an open, non-profit food database, to fetch the product’s nutrition. Nothing else is sent with it: no identifier, no account, and nothing from your diary. Open Food Facts’ own privacy policy covers how they handle requests.

Siri. If you use Vessel through Siri, Apple processes what you say under Apple’s privacy policy. Vessel receives only the resulting text, and handles it like anything typed in the app.

That is the complete list.

Permissions

PermissionUsed for
MicrophoneLogging by voice, only between tapping the microphone and tapping it again.
Speech recognitionTurning your voice into text on the device. Vessel requires on-device recognition and won’t listen at all if your device can’t provide it.
Camera and photosReading food, packaging text and barcodes from photos you choose. Images are processed on the device and not uploaded.
NotificationsThe streak reminder you turn on in Settings. Scheduled on the device.

Every permission is optional. Vessel works without any of them; you just type instead.

Health information

What you log about food and symptoms is health information, which is why Vessel never collects it. It isn’t used for advertising, sold, shared, or used to train anything. The language model inside Vessel was trained before release on generated sentences, not on anyone’s diary.

Children

Vessel collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting Vessel from your device deletes everything it stored there. Remove any backup files from the folder you chose, and any exports you made, the same way you would any other file.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. A change that meant collecting data would be announced in the app before it took effect.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/shankar-sachin/vessel/issues, or see Support.
